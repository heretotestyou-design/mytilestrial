## space-templates

These previews were made with the built-in image generation tool. Each room was generated as a contact sheet, then split into individual WebP photos at `space-templates/<space>/<template>.webp`.

Each generation used this shared prompt followed by the corresponding scene below.

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural
Create a production asset for a tile visualizer: one landscape contact sheet of EXACTLY 16 beautiful architectural-interior photographs in a precise four columns by four rows regular edge-to-edge grid. Every card the same size and horizontal 4:3. Image aspect ratio 4:3. A simple hard cut between panels, no lines, dividers, gutter, rounded corners, margins, words, numbers, graphics or labels. This will be cut at exactly 25%, 50% and 75% in both directions. Each miniature photograph fills its entire grid cell independently. Professional real-estate photography. Consistent clean architecture and broad naturally lit room in all 16 cells. Straight grout lines and realistic individual tiles. Use neutral pale base surfaces for contrast with accent tiles. Vary angle moderately only to help the tile application show. No people. No rugs in any panel. Keep the room recognizable at thumbnail size. Across the sheet, the single type of room below is always recognizable. Required tile layout in each cell, left to right:
Row 1: 1 Entire floor covered in accent tiles; 2 Neutral floor with a small 2 by 2 accent tile group at each corner; 3 Neutral floor with rectangular inset accent tile panel centered in the floor; 4 continuous accent tile border around neutral floor center.
Row 2: 1 alternating deep accent and pale neutral checkerboard floor; 2 one selected side wall tiled in accent tile, other walls neutral; 3 every visible wall tiled in accent tile, neutral floor; 4 only the back wall facing the camera tiled in accent tile, both side walls neutral.
Row 3: 1 only the left wall tiled in accent tile; 2 only the right wall tiled in accent tile; 3 one narrow vertical floor-to-ceiling accent tile strip centered on the back wall; 4 lower half of the visible walls tiled horizontally in accent tile, upper half painted light neutral, floor neutral.
Row 4: 1 combined treatment, accent tile panel in middle of floor and full back wall tiled in the same material; 2 premium coordinated room makeover with beautiful light stone-look porcelain floor and complementary decorative tile wall; 3 alternate attractive view of original room; 4 alternate good view.
Scene:
```

- `living-room` Contemporary living room, low cream sofa pushed to the far side, neutral accent chair, tall window and oak shelves. Keep the foreground floor open. Sage green accent ceramic tiles.
- `kitchen` Contemporary residential kitchen, warm oak and taupe cabinetry, countertop and sink, partly visible appliances, wide foreground and two visible walls. Petrol-blue accent ceramic tiles.
- `bedroom` Calm contemporary bedroom, upholstered bed pushed to one side of the frame, bedside table and curtains, generous visible floor and walls. Dusty blue accent ceramic tiles.
- `dining-room` Contemporary dining room, slim walnut dining table and chairs placed toward the far side, pendant light and windows, ample foreground. Deep olive accent ceramic tiles.
- `bathroom` Modern bathroom, freestanding bath, floating vanity and clear shower, both the floor and main wall strongly visible. Muted teal accent ceramic tiles.
- `office` Refined modern office, clean desk and upholstered chair in the rear corner, wood shelves and tall windows, broad open floor. Deep slate blue accent ceramic tiles.
- `outdoor` Outdoor terrace courtyard with planters, seating pushed back and a pergola, clearly outside under daylight, terrace floor and surrounding masonry visible. Terracotta accent outdoor tiles.
- `entryway` Modern residential entryway, unmistakable front door, narrow bench or console to one side, wide view of the tiled floor and surrounding walls. Moss green accent ceramic tiles.
